# BAS Daily Office

Free Morning and Evening Prayer from the *Book of Alternative Services* of the Anglican Church of Canada, built for any date, with the readings, psalm, canticles and collect that suit the day. It runs in a web browser, installs like an app, and works without internet once it has been opened.

**Open it here: https://nholehouse.github.io/BAS-daily-office/**

## What it does

- Builds Morning or Evening Prayer for any date, following the BAS calendar and its rules of precedence: Sundays and the Revised Common Lectionary, Holy Days, memorials, and the seasons.
- Chooses the introductory response, antiphon, light thanksgiving and responsory to suit the day or season. Every choice can be changed.
- Reads the service aloud, with a second voice for the readings and the prayers of the people.
- Gives you the words alone as a text file (for a voice or audio app), a Word file, or a printout.

## Installing it

You can use it straight from the link. Installing puts an icon on your device and lets it open without internet.

| Device | Browser | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android phone or tablet | Chrome | Tap **Install**, or the ⋮ menu → **Install app** (or **Add to home screen**) |
| iPhone or iPad | Safari | Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add** |
| Windows PC | Chrome or Edge | Click **Install**, or the install icon in the address bar. Edge: ⋯ → **Apps → Install this site as an app** |
| Mac | Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer) | **File → Add to Dock**. Chrome or Edge: as on Windows |

If the link opens inside another app (Facebook, WhatsApp, an email app), choose **Open in Chrome** or **Open in Safari** first. Firefox on a computer does not install it in the usual way; use Chrome or Edge.

*Tested on Android and in desktop browsers. The iPhone and Mac steps are Apple's standard ones and have not yet been tried.*

## First use

On the first start the app offers the free Bible texts for the readings. Tap **Install both Bibles** (about 3 MB, once): the King James Version with Apocrypha, and the World English Bible, British Edition. After that they work offline.

**NRSV.** The NRSV is copyrighted, so it is not included. If you want it, open ☰ → **Bible texts**. Follow the link there to get a PDF, then tap **Build NRSV from my PDF…** and choose the file. It takes a few seconds to a minute, happens on your own device, and nothing is uploaded. The app only accepts the PDF layout it was built for, and checks its own result.

## Looking after your copy

- Each device keeps its own copy of the app, its settings and its Bibles. Nothing is shared between devices.
- Clearing your browser's **cookies and other site data** removes the app, your settings and the Bibles. Clearing only browsing history does not. If it happens, open the link while online and reinstall the Bibles.
- When a new version is published, a bar appears with **Restart now**.

## Privacy

The app sends nothing anywhere and has no tracking. Settings and Bibles are stored only in your browser. The only network requests are loading the app and downloading the Bible texts from this same site, plus the PDF link above, which opens a page on the Internet Archive.
